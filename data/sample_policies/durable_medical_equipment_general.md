# Coverage Policy: Durable Medical Equipment (General)

Policy ID: CP-DME-2024-002
Effective: 2024-01-01
Owner: DME Coverage Committee
Audience: internal

## 1. Scope

Coverage of durable medical equipment (DME), prosthetics, orthotics,
and related supplies except items covered under Policy CP-DME-2024-014
(CGM) which supersedes this document for CGM-specific benefits.

## 2. Definition of DME

Equipment that:

- can withstand repeated use
- is used for a medical purpose
- is generally not useful in the absence of illness or injury
- is appropriate for use in the home

## 3. Coverage Standard

Covered when prescribed by a treating physician AND documented as
medically necessary in the member's plan of care.

## 4. Non-covered Items

- Comfort or convenience items (grab bars, humidifiers unless
  respiratory)
- Environmental control units
- Home modifications (ramps, stair lifts)
- Exercise equipment

## 5. Rental vs Purchase

- Items expected to be needed < 90 days: rental
- Items expected to be needed >= 90 days: capped rental converting to
  purchase per Medicare LCD guidelines

## 6. Repair and Replacement

- Repair covered when cost-effective vs replacement
- Replacement covered when equipment is beyond useful life OR member's
  condition changes materially

## 7. Prior Authorization

Required for items > $500 acquisition cost. See PA queue routing.
