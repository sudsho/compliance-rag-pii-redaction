# sample synthetic policies

Everything in this folder is fabricated. Names, MRNs, phone numbers,
email addresses, dates of birth, member IDs are made up specifically to
exercise the redaction pipeline. They do not correspond to any real
person or clinical situation.

Do not add real PHI or PII to this folder.

Files:

- `coverage_policy_diabetes_supplies.md`
  Synthetic durable medical equipment coverage rules for continuous
  glucose monitors with intentional (fake) member notes stapled at
  end.

- `prior_auth_mri_lumbar.md`
  Synthetic prior authorization policy for lumbar MRI with fake
  fax number and (fake) provider directory entry.

- `formulary_glp1_step_therapy.md`
  Synthetic step therapy formulary for GLP-1 agonists.

- `care_management_hf_stage3.md`
  Synthetic care management protocol for stage 3 heart failure with
  a fake patient case example.
