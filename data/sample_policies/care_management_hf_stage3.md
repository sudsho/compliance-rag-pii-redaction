# Care Management Protocol: Heart Failure Stage C (NYHA III)

Policy ID: CM-CARDIO-2024-008
Effective: 2024-04-01
Audience: internal

## 1. Enrollment

Members with ICD-10 I50.x + NYHA class III symptoms + hospitalization in
the last 12 months are auto-enrolled with an opt-out letter mailed
within 5 business days of discharge notification.

## 2. Nurse Outreach Cadence

- Week 1: 2 calls (medication reconciliation, weight monitoring plan)
- Weeks 2-8: weekly calls
- Months 3-6: biweekly
- Beyond: monthly

## 3. Device Support

Members with implanted device (LVAD, CRT-D, ICD) require additional
protocol CM-CARDIO-2024-009.

## 4. Escalation Triggers

Nurse escalates to case manager if:

- Weight gain > 3 lbs in 24 hours or > 5 lbs in a week
- New or worsening dyspnea
- Missed refill of loop diuretic > 7 days

Case manager escalates to medical director for:

- Two ED visits in 30 days
- Non-adherence to guideline-directed medical therapy (ARNI + beta
  blocker + MRA + SGLT2i)

## 5. Metrics

- 30-day readmission rate
- MRA adherence
- SGLT2i adherence
- Member-reported outcome (KCCQ-12)
