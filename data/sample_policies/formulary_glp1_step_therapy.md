# Formulary Step Therapy: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists

Policy ID: RX-FORM-2024-031
Effective: 2024-03-01
Audience: internal

## 1. Scope

Step therapy for GLP-1 receptor agonists prescribed for Type 2 Diabetes.
Weight-loss-only indications are governed by a separate policy (RX-FORM-
2024-045) and are NOT covered under this document.

## 2. Preferred Agents (Step 1)

- Semaglutide (Ozempic) - subcutaneous
- Dulaglutide (Trulicity)

## 3. Step Requirement

Members must have documented trial of AND failure or intolerance to at
least one preferred agent for a minimum of 90 days before non-preferred
agents (Rybelsus oral semaglutide, Mounjaro tirzepatide) will be
covered.

## 4. Exceptions

Bypass step therapy if:

- Contraindication documented (personal or family history of medullary
  thyroid carcinoma, MEN 2 syndrome, pancreatitis).
- Prior successful use of a non-preferred agent under a prior insurer.

## 5. Quantity Limits

Manufacturer-labeled maximums.

## 6. Compounded Semaglutide

Not covered. Direct member to FDA safety communication.
