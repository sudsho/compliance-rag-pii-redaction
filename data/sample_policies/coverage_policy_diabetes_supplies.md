# Coverage Policy: Diabetes Supplies (Continuous Glucose Monitors)

Policy ID: CP-DME-2024-014
Effective: 2024-01-01
Owner: DME Coverage Committee
Audience: internal (member services, prior auth reviewers)

## 1. Scope

Coverage of Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) devices and related
supplies for adult members with Type 1 Diabetes and insulin-dependent
Type 2 Diabetes.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

Member must meet ALL of the following:

- Confirmed diagnosis of T1DM or insulin-dependent T2DM (ICD-10 E10.x or
  E11.x with concomitant insulin use).
- At least 3 injections of insulin per day OR use of an insulin pump.
- Adherent to self-monitoring blood glucose testing.
- Prescribed by an endocrinologist or PCP with diabetes management
  experience.

## 3. Covered Devices

- Dexcom G6, G7
- FreeStyle Libre 2, 3
- Medtronic Guardian 4

## 4. Prior Authorization

Required for initial fill. Refills within the same benefit year require
verification of continued insulin use but do NOT require re-review.

## 5. Quantity Limits

- Sensors: 3 per 28-day supply
- Transmitters: as manufacturer specification

## 6. Denial and Appeals

Deny with reason code DME-014-INELIGIBLE if criteria in section 2 are not
met. Appeal window: 60 days.
