# Prior Authorization Policy: Lumbar MRI

Policy ID: PA-IMG-2024-022
Effective: 2024-02-01
Audience: internal
Contact fax (fake): 617-555-0198

## 1. Scope

Prior authorization required for elective outpatient MRI of the lumbar
spine (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158).

## 2. Approval Criteria

Approve if any of:

- Radicular pain > 6 weeks despite conservative therapy (PT, NSAIDs).
- Progressive neurological deficit (motor weakness, cauda equina signs).
- Suspected malignancy (unexplained weight loss, night pain, history of
  cancer).
- Suspected infection (fever, IVDU history, immunocompromised).
- Post-surgical evaluation < 90 days from procedure.

## 3. Denial Criteria

Deny with reason PA-IMG-022-CONS if member has NOT completed 6 weeks of
conservative therapy AND does NOT meet any red-flag criterion.

## 4. Turnaround

Standard: 5 business days.
Urgent: 24 hours (requires physician attestation).

## 5. Appeal

Peer-to-peer within 10 business days of denial.
